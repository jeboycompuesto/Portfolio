01 / The product

Turn a business question into a clearer decision.

A bank balance shows what you have today. A hiring or expansion decision depends on what happens next: setup costs, monthly spending, and when new revenue arrives.

Finance Bro AI brings those assumptions into a guided flow, so founders can compare scenarios and understand the reasoning behind a recommendation.

  1. 01

    Ask the question

    Start with a concrete plan: can we fund a NYC team?

  2. 02

    Make assumptions visible

    Set costs, revenue timing, and the cash reserve to protect.

  3. 03

    Explore the trade-offs

    Adjust the plan and see cash projections and recommendations update.
